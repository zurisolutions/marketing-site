Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who is responsible

Zuri Solutions (company currently being registered) is responsible for the processing of your personal data. Contact: contact@zurisolutions.dev

What we collect

Only what you send us yourself:

  • through the contact, training and diagnostic forms: your name, email address, company (optional) and message;
  • through the diagnostic: your answers to the 9 questions and the resulting score;
  • when you email us: your email address and the content of your message.

Why we use it

To answer your request, set up the call or audit you asked for, and send you the training program. We do not sell your data and do not send you marketing emails without your consent.

Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (article 6.1.b of the GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us (article 6.1.f).

Who processes it

  • Google (Google Forms and Google Sheets): receives and stores the forms you send.
  • GitHub (website hosting): may record technical data such as your IP address, for security.
  • Google Fonts: the website's fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Booking tool, if you use it to book a call: it processes the information you enter under its own privacy policy.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Up to 3 years after our last contact with you, unless you become a client, in which case the rules applying to our business relationship take over.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or limit its use, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Just email contact@zurisolutions.dev. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Cookies

This website sets no advertising cookies and no audience measurement cookies. No cookie is needed to browse it, which is why we don't show a cookie banner.

  • Google Fonts sets no cookie, but receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • The booking tool, when you open it, may set the cookies it needs to work.

If we ever add an audience measurement tool that requires your consent, we will ask for it first.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version published on this page applies.
