What we build

Custom tools, built around how your team actually works.

ai-agents

AI agents

Assistants trained on your documents and tools. They look things up, draft answers and prepare the work, and a person approves before anything is sent.

Question or email → Zuri → Draft to approve

Typical examples

  • Answer recurring customer or supplier questions from your own documents.
  • Draft replies to incoming emails, ready for a person to check and send.
  • Find information across files, emails and tools with one question.
workflows

Workflow automation

The hand-offs between your tools happen on their own: no more copying, re-typing or chasing. Every rule is agreed on with your team.

New order → Zuri → Every tool updated

Typical examples

  • A new order is created in every tool that needs it, automatically.
  • Approval requests go straight to the right person.
  • Reminders and follow-ups are sent on time, without anyone thinking about it.
documents

Invoices, delivery notes, forms, quotes: the AI reads them, extracts what matters and fills your tools, or drafts the document for you.

Document → Zuri → Clean data

  • Check documents against your rules before they go out.
