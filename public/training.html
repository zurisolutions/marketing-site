Why train your team

Everyone has heard of AI. Few use it well.

  • Some use it. Most don’t.

    A few people have figured it out on their own. Everyone else still does things the long way.

  • Nobody knows what’s allowed.

    Can we paste client data into an AI assistant? Without clear rules, people either take risks or stay away.

  • Paid subscriptions, same old habits.

    The licences are there. The day-to-day work hasn’t changed.

The program

Three modules, from first prompt to first automation.

Every module is adapted to your team: your tools, your documents, your recurring tasks. No generic examples.

  1. Module 01

    Use AI every day

    Prompting, drafting, summarizing and analyzing with AI assistants, safely and effectively.

    Your team leaves able to
    • Write clear prompts that get useful answers.
    • Draft, summarize and rewrite emails and documents.
    • Analyze files and data with an AI assistant.
  2. Module 02

    Build your own automations

    Connect your tools and automate recurring tasks, step by step.

    Your team leaves able to
    • Spot the tasks worth automating.
    • Connect two tools so information moves on its own.
    • Build and test a first automation on a real task.
  3. Module 03

    Make it stick

    Real use cases from your own workflows, good practices and data security rules.

    Your team leaves able to
    • Apply AI to the team’s own cases.
    • Know which data can go into AI tools, and which can’t.
    • Share the same good practices.

Built for your team, not off the shelf.
